## Design Context

### Users

The landing page is for developers, technical founders, and non-technical site owners evaluating the Payload + Astro starter. They need to understand what it does, how to begin, and where to inspect the source without reading a product manual.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, exact, and calm. It should feel like WAYF's website translated into a dark technical surface: confident, useful, and free of developer-tool theatre.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark-only editorial minimalism. Warm near-black surfaces, warm white type, one sparingly used magenta accent, 1px structural rails, square geometry, and one shared 14–15px type size. The hero statement is the single size exception. Hierarchy elsewhere comes from weight, spacing, case, and opacity. Glass is reserved for the navigation and setup command where depth communicates function.

### Design Principles

- Make the first action and the repository immediately discoverable.
- Use one fluid font size everywhere except the hero statement, whose display scale creates the page's primary contrast.
- Prefer structural lines and whitespace over cards, badges, and decorative effects.
- Keep glass rare, subtle, and tied to interactive or command surfaces.
- Show the WAYF mark as restrained navigation branding, never as a floating or promotional widget.
- Preserve the starter's concrete copy: its workflow, skills, stack, setup requirements, and safety model are the substance of the page.
