## Design Context

### Users

The landing page is for developers, technical founders, and non-technical site owners evaluating the Payload + Astro starter. They need to understand what it does, how to begin, and where to inspect the source without reading a product manual.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, exact, and calm. It should feel like WAYF's website translated into a dark technical surface: confident, useful, and free of developer-tool theatre.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark-only editorial minimalism. Warm near-black surfaces, warm white type, one sparingly used magenta accent, 1px structural rails, square geometry, and one shared 1rem type size. Hierarchy comes from weight, spacing, case, and opacity. Glass is reserved for the navigation and setup command where depth communicates function.

### Design Principles

- Make the first action and the repository immediately discoverable.
- Use one font size everywhere; establish hierarchy without scaling type.
- Prefer structural lines and whitespace over cards, badges, and decorative effects.
- Keep glass rare, subtle, and tied to interactive or command surfaces.
- Remove branded widgets; credit WAYF with normal text and links.
